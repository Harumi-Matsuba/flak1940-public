Use ONLY the attached images. Do not run any shell commands and do not open any files.

The images are strips cut from one teleprinter (telex) page of 1940. The page holds a cipher text typed in capital letters in five-letter groups. Each text line was cut into a left strip and a right strip; the images are given in reading order (line 1 left, line 1 right, line 2 left, line 2 right, ...). The last line may be shorter, and its last group may have fewer than five letters.

A later reader marked the page with a pen: short dashes or arcs under letters, strokes through letters, loops and slashes. These pen marks are not part of the typed text.

Transcribe every typed letter group exactly as typed, including groups that are struck through (for a letter that is completely blotted out write ?). Notes on this typeface:
- The typed Q has a short tail inside the lower right of its bowl. A pen dash under an O is not a tail.
- The typed I is a plain vertical bar without serifs.
- If you are unsure between two letters, write both separated by a slash, e.g. O/Q, first your best guess.

Output only the groups, one text line per output line (join the left and right strip of a line), groups separated by spaces. Do not add commentary.
